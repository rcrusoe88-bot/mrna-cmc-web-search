## 3. Specialist watch by workflow

### IVT and drug substance

- IVT yield and productivity
- co-transcriptional or post-transcriptional capping
- dsRNA impurity reduction
- downstream purification
- TFF, chromatography, and concentration steps
- raw-material strategy and comparability

### LNP and drug product

- targeted LNP composition
- microfluidic mixing and scale-up
- encapsulation efficiency
- particle characterization
- biodistribution and targeting data
- stability and freeze-thaw performance

### Analytical and quality methods

- capping-efficiency methods
- sequence or identity mapping
- impurity methods
- release testing
- stability-indicating methods
- orthogonal CQA strategies

## 6. What counts as a signal

Do not track everything. Prioritize items that can support a useful research angle.

High-signal examples:

- new conference abstract with differentiated formulation, process, or assay route
- peer-reviewed paper with clear platform, process, or analytical inflection
- regulatory guidance affecting development or CMC expectations
- company disclosure of manufacturing simplification, assay strategy, raw-material strategy, or cost advantages

Low-signal examples:

- generic corporate milestone language
- duplicate syndication of the same press release
- review articles without a fresh framing angle

## 7. Weekly monitoring routine

1. Check the highest-priority recurring watch list.
2. Check the workflow-specific specialist watch before broad company scanning.
3. Check whether any major meeting is in abstract-release or live-conference season.
4. Pull the newest relevant journal items from the journal watch table.
5. Check company pages only after a platform, process, or meeting signal appears.
6. Package only the strongest items into a concise brief with direct links.

## 8. Monthly deep-dive routine

1. Cluster the month's signals into themes.
2. Identify recurring bottlenecks in IVT, purification, formulation, stability, analytics, in vivo delivery, and CMC.
3. Explicitly separate sequence design, IVT, purification, formulation, in vivo delivery, analytical strategy, and manufacturing readiness.
4. Compare what conferences, papers, regulators, and companies are saying about the same problem.
5. Recommend research themes rather than publication copy.
