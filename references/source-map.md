# Source Map

Use this file when you need a fast shortlist of high-value sources for mRNA IVT, mRNA drug substance preparation, targeted LNP, mRNA-LNP formulation, in vivo CAR-T, in vivo cell engineering, analytical methods, and broader CMC or process-development material discovery.

## 1. Official conferences and meetings

Start here when the user wants the latest signals, abstracts, or annual-meeting coverage.

### Core RNA, formulation, and advanced-therapy meetings

- ASGCT Annual Meeting
- ISCT Annual Meeting
- ESGCT Annual Congress
- AAPS conferences and workshops
- RNA Development, CMC & Manufacturing Summit
- RNA Leaders USA
- TIDES USA

### Adjacent oncology and translational meetings

- AACR Annual Meeting
- ASCO Annual Meeting
- SITC Annual Meeting
- ASH Annual Meeting
- EHA Congress

### Advanced-therapy industry events

- Advanced Therapies or Phacilitate-branded meetings
- Manufacturing-focused RNA, biologics, or cell and gene therapy events with official agenda and speaker pages

For each meeting, look for:

- abstract portal
- online planner or agenda
- late-breaking abstract release
- oral or poster session titles
- exhibitor announcements
- company satellite or investor presentations released around the meeting

## 3. Regulatory and standards sources

- U.S. FDA
- EMA
- NMPA
- CDE China
- ICH when cross-region technical-guidance context is useful

Look for:

- official guidance documents
- quality or CMC technical notes
- manufacturing and control expectations
- analytical-validation expectations
- stability, comparability, or raw-material expectations

## 4. Company and market sources

Use these for platform movements, manufacturing strategy, raw-material developments, assay technologies, and conference-adjacent disclosures.

### Best first stops

- company newsroom or press releases
- investor relations
- SEC filings for public companies
- company presentation decks released at conferences or earnings calls

### Typical company categories to track

- IVT reagent and raw-material suppliers
- capping, enzyme, and nucleotide suppliers
- LNP delivery companies
- targeted LNP platform companies
- in vivo CAR-T platform developers
- in vivo cell-engineering companies
- mRNA manufacturing and CDMO ecosystem participants
- assay, analytics, formulation, or closed-system manufacturing providers

## 5. Discovery platforms

Use these to discover material quickly, then trace back to the underlying primary source before packaging.

- PubMed
- Google Scholar
- arXiv

Do not treat discovery platforms as a substitute for source verification.

## 6. Evidence ladder

Use this evidence ranking when packaging results.

1. Peer-reviewed paper or official regulator document
2. Official conference abstract or oral presentation listing
3. Company technical deck, methods poster, or investor presentation with usable detail
4. Company press release
5. Secondary media report

If evidence is level 3, 4, or 5, say so explicitly.
